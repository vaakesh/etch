# Etch: create a card
<!-- chunk_id: etch.cards.create -->

Open “Library” → “Cards” and select “New card”. Fill in “Foreign word”, “Translation”, “Word language”, and “Translation language”, then select “Create”.

Both sides need non-empty text of up to 255 characters after trimming leading and trailing whitespace. A language code contains 2–3 Latin letters, such as `en`, `ru`, or `de`. Do not enter full language names in the code fields.

The new card appears in your card library. Use “Add to deck” to include it in a deck for practice.

# Etch: add a card to one or more decks
<!-- chunk_id: etch.cards.assign -->

In “Library” → “Cards”, open a card's “Actions” menu and select “Add to deck”. Select one or more of your own decks and choose “Save”.

This adds the existing card to the selected decks. The interface tells you if the card is already in a deck. You cannot add your cards to someone else's saved deck.

A deck cannot contain more than 5000 cards. When adding a card to several decks, successful changes may remain saved even if another operation fails. Check the messages beside the decks and retry only the failed actions.

# Etch: edit a card used in several decks
<!-- chunk_id: etch.cards.edit -->

In your card library, select “Actions” → “Edit” for your card. Change its texts or language codes and select “Save”. You can also edit your cards in the deck editor and on the deck page.

If the same card belongs to several decks, editing changes the card itself, so all those decks show the changes. Create separate cards to keep different versions of the text.

An existing practice session stores a snapshot of its texts. Editing a card does not rewrite the questions or expected answers in that session. Start a new session to practice the updated text.

# Etch: remove a card from just one deck
<!-- chunk_id: etch.cards.detach -->

Open “Edit deck”. Find the card in the deck contents and choose “Actions” → “Remove from deck”.

This removes the card's association with the selected deck. The card remains in your card library and any other decks it belongs to. You can add it again from the card library later.

Use “Remove from deck” in the editor when you want to keep the card itself. The “Delete card” button on the regular deck page permanently deletes the card.

# Etch: permanently delete a card
<!-- chunk_id: etch.cards.delete -->

To permanently delete your card, use its delete action in the library's “Cards” tab or “Delete card” on a deck page.

Permanent deletion removes the card from your card library and all active decks containing it. To remove the word from just one deck, open the deck editor and choose “Remove from deck”.

Existing practice sessions keep snapshots of card texts. Seeing an old word in session history does not mean the deleted card still exists in your current library.
