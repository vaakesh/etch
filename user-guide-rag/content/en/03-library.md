# Etch: decks and cards in your library
<!-- chunk_id: etch.library.contents -->

In the Etch “Library”, the “Decks” tab shows your own decks and other users' decks that you have saved. The “Cards” tab contains your own cards.

A card can exist without a deck or belong to several of your decks. Saving someone else's public deck does not make its cards yours or give you permission to edit them.

Select a deck title to view its contents. Use “Show more” when more entries are available. An empty “Cards” tab does not necessarily mean your saved decks from other users contain no words.

# Etch: search for decks and cards
<!-- chunk_id: etch.library.search -->

To find a deck, open “Library” → “Decks” and enter part of its title in “Search decks”. To find a word, open the “Cards” tab and use “Find a word or translation”.

Card search checks the text on both sides. Search queries are limited to 50 characters. The separate search in “Catalog” finds public decks by title.

If nothing matches, shorten the query, check the spelling, and turn off “Favorites only” if enabled. Clear the search field to return to the full list.

# Etch: favorite decks and library layout
<!-- chunk_id: etch.library.favourites -->

In your library, use “Add to favorites” for a deck. “Favorites only” displays your favorite decks. “Remove from favorites” clears the mark without deleting the deck.

You can favorite your own deck immediately. Save someone else's public deck to your library before adding it to favorites. Removing a deck from your saved decks also removes its favorite mark.

The “Deck layout” control switches between “Full view” and “Compact view”. It does not change cards, deck access, or practice results.
