# Etch: find and save a public deck
<!-- chunk_id: etch.catalog.save -->

Open “Catalog” and search for a deck by title. Use “Preview” to see its cards, or select its title or “Open deck” to open the full page.

Select “Save” on someone else's public deck to add it to your library. Your own deck displays “In library” and does not need to be saved again.

You can practice with a public deck while it is available. Saving it to your library first is not required to start a practice session.

# Etch: what saving someone else's deck means
<!-- chunk_id: etch.catalog.saved-reference -->

The regular “Save” action in the Etch catalog adds access to the author's deck to your library. It does not create a separate personal copy. The author still controls its contents, and later views of the deck show the author's updates.

You cannot edit someone else's saved deck as your own. If the author deletes it or makes it private, regular access may disappear. Check “Deck archive”: an available archive entry can be restored as your own private copy before its deadline.

A practice session that has already started uses the card snapshot created when that session began.

# Etch: remove a deck from your saved decks
<!-- chunk_id: etch.catalog.unsave -->

In the catalog or author's profile, use the saved deck's “Saved” control or the action to remove it from saved decks.

The deck is removed from your saved list, and its favorite mark is cleared. The author's original deck is not deleted, and other users can continue using it while they have access.

Removing a saved deck does not delete your practice history. If the deck is still public, you can save it again from the catalog.

# Etch: user profiles and an author's decks
<!-- chunk_id: etch.catalog.profile -->

Select the author's username on a deck to open their profile. Profiles use `/users/username` and require signing in to Etch.

A profile shows the username, registration date, number of decks, and deck saves. Your own profile includes your private decks; someone else's profile shows their public decks.

From a profile, you can open or preview a deck, save another user's public deck, or start practice. “Show more” loads the next part of the list. You can also open your own profile through your username in the sidebar.
