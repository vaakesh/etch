# Etch: change the interface language
<!-- chunk_id: etch.settings.language -->

Open “Settings” (`/settings`). Under “Language”, choose `ru (Russian)` or `en (English)`, then select “Save”.

“Cancel” discards unsaved form changes. “Unsaved changes” means the settings still need to be saved; “Saved” confirms success.

The interface language changes Etch's labels and messages. Card texts and languages are configured separately in the card editor; changing the interface language does not translate them.

# Etch: appearance and long text previews
<!-- chunk_id: etch.settings.appearance -->

In Etch “Settings”, “Appearance” offers “Light”, “Dark”, and “System” themes. The system theme follows your device or browser's appearance preference. Select “Save” after choosing a theme.

“Long text previews” controls whether supported interface elements show their full text on hover or focus. Enable it when long words, translations, or descriptions are truncated in lists.

Appearance settings do not change card contents or practice results.
