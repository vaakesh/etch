# Etch: which decks appear in the archive
<!-- chunk_id: etch.archive.purpose -->

“Deck archive” holds snapshots available to you after access to a deck changes. Reasons include deleting the deck, deleting the author's account, or making a public deck private.

An archive is created for eligible users connected to the deck through saving or practice. Do not assume every deck you have ever viewed will appear in your archive.

Open “Library” → “Decks” → “Deck archive” (`/library/decks/archive`). Each entry shows a reason and an “Available until” date. The current retention period for a new archive is 30 days; use the date shown for the specific entry.

# Etch: restore a deck from the archive
<!-- chunk_id: etch.archive.restore -->

Open “Deck archive”. For the entry you need, select “View cards” to inspect it, then “Restore” before the “Available until” deadline.

Restoration creates a separate private deck in your library and transfers the progress and sessions associated with that archive. You own this copy and can manage it as your own deck. The author's original deck and its public access are not restored.

After restoration, use “Open copy”. Restoring the same archive entry again does not create another copy.

<!-- RAG_CHUNK -->

# Etch: an archive expired or its restored copy was deleted
<!-- chunk_id: etch.archive.unavailable -->

After the “Available until” deadline, an archived deck cannot be viewed or restored for the first time. Restore materials you need before the deadline.

If you already restored a personal copy from an archive entry and then deleted it, that same entry cannot be restored again. The interface reports: “The saved copy was deleted. This deck cannot be restored again.”

A practice session linked to an archived deck cannot continue directly on the archive. If restoration is still available, restore the deck first, then try continuing the transferred session through “Practice”.
