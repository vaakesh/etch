# Etch: what the service does
<!-- chunk_id: etch.overview.purpose -->

Etch helps you learn vocabulary with flashcards. Each card contains two texts and their languages, such as “привет” and “hello”. A deck groups cards by topic.

You can create decks, publish them, save other users' public decks, and practice by typing answers. Etch stores practice sessions, answer history, and study metrics.

You must sign in to access the library, catalog, practice, and profiles. A public deck is available to other signed-in users.

# Etch: start your first practice session with your own deck
<!-- chunk_id: etch.overview.first-practice -->

1. Register or sign in to Etch.
2. Open “Library” → “Decks” and select “New deck”.
3. Enter a title and create the deck.
4. Create a card in the “Cards” tab, then use its “Add to deck” action to select your deck. You can also use JSON “Import” instead of entering cards manually.
5. Select “Start practice” for the deck.
6. Choose the direction and number of cards in session setup, then start practicing.

A deck needs at least one card for practice. If you do not have your own materials, you can find a public deck in “Catalog”.

# Etch: find the main sections
<!-- chunk_id: etch.overview.navigation -->

The Etch sidebar provides “Home”, “Library”, “Catalog”, “Practice”, and “Settings”.

- “Home” (`/`) shows decks and study metrics.
- “Library” contains the “Decks” (`/library/decks`) and “Cards” (`/library/cards`) tabs.
- “Catalog” (`/catalog`) contains public decks.
- “Practice” (`/practice`) lists practice sessions.
- “Settings” (`/settings`) lets you change the language and appearance.

Open your profile by selecting your username in the sidebar. The “Statistics” section is not connected yet; use the home page for study metrics.
