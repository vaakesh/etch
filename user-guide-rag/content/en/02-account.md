# Etch: register an account
<!-- chunk_id: etch.account.register -->

On `/login`, select “Register”, enter a username, email, and password, then select “Create account”.

- Username: 3–18 characters; Latin letters, digits, `_`, and `-`.
- Email: an address in a valid format.
- Password: 8–128 characters with no whitespace.

The Etch form identifies the service as a demo: use made-up details and a separate password that you do not use on other websites.

After registration, Etch attempts to sign you in automatically. If the account was created but automatic sign-in failed, switch to “Sign in” and use the username and password you just created.

# Etch: sign in and complete the bot check
<!-- chunk_id: etch.account.login -->

Open `/login`, select “Sign in”, enter your username and password, and select “Sign in”. Use your username in the login field, not your email address.

The browser automatically completes the bot check during sign-in and registration. If you see “We couldn't complete the bot check”, submit the form again.

If the username or password is incorrect, check what you entered. If registration reports that the username or email is already taken, use different details or sign in to your existing account.

# Etch: sign out or handle an expired session
<!-- chunk_id: etch.account.logout -->

Open your profile through your username in the sidebar, select “Sign out”, and confirm. “Stay signed in” cancels the action.

Signing out ends your current authentication session. Your account's decks and progress remain saved. Sign in to the same account to continue studying.

“Your session expired. Sign in again.” means you need to enter your username and password again. Session expiry alone does not mean your study data was deleted.
